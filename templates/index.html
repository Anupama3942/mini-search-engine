Search Syntax

  • " ": Phrase Search. Require consecutive words. (e.g. "machine learning")
  • AND: Both terms must be present. (e.g. python AND programming)
  • OR: At least one term must be present. (e.g. python OR java)
  • NOT: Exclude documents containing the term. (e.g. NOT java)
  • ( ): Control the order of evaluation. (e.g. (python OR java) AND programming)
  • Precedence: NOT → AND → OR

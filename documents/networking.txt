Computer networking is the practice of connecting computers and devices to share data and resources.
The Internet is the largest example of a computer network, connecting billions of devices worldwide.
Networks use protocols such as TCP/IP, HTTP, and DNS to communicate and transfer data reliably.
A Local Area Network (LAN) connects devices within a small area like a home or office.
A Wide Area Network (WAN) connects devices across large geographical distances.
Routers, switches, and firewalls are common networking hardware used to manage traffic and security.
Understanding networking is important for web development, cloud computing, and cybersecurity.
Network programming in languages like Python and Java allows developers to build client-server applications.
